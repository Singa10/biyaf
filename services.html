Studio / Services

What We Design

From first sketch to final inspection — Biyaf offers full architectural services for residential, commercial and public-sector clients.

A—01

Architectural Design

Full concept-to-construction design services — from feasibility studies and concept massing through to detailed construction drawings, for homes, offices, retail and institutional buildings.

A—02

Interior Architecture

Spatial planning, material palettes, lighting design and custom joinery that carry a building's architectural language through to its interior spaces.

A—03

Urban & Masterplanning

Site strategy, density and massing studies, and full masterplans for mixed-use and multi-phase developments.

A—04

Renovation & Restoration

Adaptive reuse, structural renewal and sensitive restoration for existing buildings and heritage sites.

A—05

Construction Administration

On-site oversight through the build phase, coordinating contractors and engineers to keep a project true to its drawings.

01
